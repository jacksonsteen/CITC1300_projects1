What are Editing Skills?

Most Popular Apps for Personal & Professional Use

The most popular apps that are professional standard are either Adobe Premiere Pro or After Effects. Both of these apps provide a different specialty. With expertise in After Effects you would want to improve on things like Motion Graphics or VFX. With expertise in Premiere Pro you want to determine an efficient workflow for things like long-form Video Editing; specifically clean transitions, pacing, and understanding of seperating good scenes/moments from boring or unneeded ones.

Good Sources to Improve Editing Skill

The best ways you can improve editing skill are split between a few choices. First, use tutorials from a site like Youtube which has an answer to basically any editing question you could have. It spans from beginner tips to learn apps like After Effects and Premiere Pro, to tips on finding sources of income through editing, and even to advanced things like visual effects tutorials or 3d creation apps like Blender. Also, going into these softwares and using various effects or practicing various things like clean motion and design will help you form your own style. It is vitally important not to strictly copy and follow tutorials, but to learn the basics from these tutorials and apply it in your own way.
