Most Important Things to Know About After Effects

The first thing you should be aware of is that, while it has the capability, After Effects is not the most efficient software to edit long-form videos such as things over about 5 minutes. It is much better used to manipulate effects, create and manipulate 3d scenes, and stylize. If you are looking to cut audio and video, a much smarter choice would be to do so in Premiere Pro while using Adobe Dynamic Link to do your visual effect/scene movement in After Effects. Overall, After Effects is a very complex, yet important, editing program that takes a lot of time to master, but the results are certainly worth it.
