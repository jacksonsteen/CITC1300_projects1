Most Important Things to Know About Premiere Pro

Like After Effects, Premiere Pro has it's strengths and weaknesses. Where Premiere Pro is a very powerful software for sifting through hours of footage and audio to combine it into one collective project, it does lack in creating and manipulating visual effects. Also, Premiere Pro has little 3d capabilities. For these reasons it is used, like After Effects, as a tool to combine with the other softeare to use it's full potential.
